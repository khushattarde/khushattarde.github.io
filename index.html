Khush Attarde

Computer Vision · Robotics

I am a Computer Vision Engineer (R&D) at Ogmen Robotics, where I develop perception models for understanding pet and animal behavior.

My broader research goal is to build manipulation systems that generalize, robots that learn from perception (visual and tactile) without dense supervision or large datasets. I am drawn to the low-data regime: sample-efficient policies that transfer across environments and handle novel tasks with minimal prior exposure.

A longer-term interest is in understanding what makes humans such capable general-purpose agents, and translating those principles into robotic learning.

I completed my B.Tech in Robotics and Automation from Symbiosis Institute of Technology, Pune in 2025, and interned at Ogmen Robotics for a year prior to joining full-time.
